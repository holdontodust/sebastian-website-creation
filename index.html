Google visibility • How it works

Showing up on Google is upkeep, not magic

When someone searches for your trade in your town, Google shows the businesses that look clean, relevant, active, and proven. That is the work I do every week.

1

Clean means trust

Right phone, right hours, right services, a site that loads and a form that works. Messy or old info makes Google, and customers, pick someone else.

2

Right words mean relevance

Each service gets its own page or section with the exact words people search, like a service plus a town. One clear page per job and town.

3

Active stays visible

Fresh photos and posts signal your business is alive. Between two similar companies, Google tends to show the one that posted last week.

4

Reviews are proof

Count, rating, and replies. I reply to every new review, keep your profile checked weekly for flags, and send you a short monthly report of what was done.

The honest part

I will never promise you a ranking or a number one spot on Google. Nobody honest can, Google decides that. What I can promise is the work gets done every week and every month, and you see it in a plain English report.

No. Care and Everything Handled are month to month, and the first month is free.
